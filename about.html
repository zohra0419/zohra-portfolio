What sets me apart

Craft + collaboration

Design Systems

Experience creating design systems from scratch, cleaning up existing design systems, transferring from Sketch to Figma, managing process and upkeep, and collaborating with developers for code consistency.

Advanced Figma Skills

Organized and skilled with complex components, variables, advanced prototyping including conditionals and logic, modes, and animations.

User Research & Empathy

Extensive practice and studies of research methods, limiting biases, and processing insights. I think through scenarios and use cases from the users’ perspective to uncover potential pain points and assumptions, and use them to advocate for user research.

Clear Communication

I have a knack for simplifying complex concepts. I provide enough context for any stakeholder to jump in and understand what’s going on. When communicating designs, I show prototypes whenever possible and point out benefits using known principles and research.

UX & UI Design

When brainstorming solutions, I start from current challenges and think from many angles. I bring requirements together and explore multiple solutions for low-effort MVP and ideal future states. For UI design, I enjoy current design trends and collect patterns that help me think out of the box.
